About me

I’m building a new career in IT and cybersecurity in Auckland, bringing twenty years of practical troubleshooting, technical field service and experience working with customers.

My background

I’m originally from France, and I’ve spent most of the past twenty years in electrical work and field service. My work involved finding faults, making repairs and helping customers: understanding their needs, finding suitable solutions and putting them into practice.

Since November 2025, I’ve been studying IT and cybersecurity, earning CompTIA A+, Network+ and Security+. I’m currently working towards the EC-Council CEH certification. Alongside my studies, I use guided labs and a personal virtual lab to practise networking, Linux and security fundamentals.

Why cybersecurity

I’ve always been passionate about computing: what happens behind my screen, where the data goes and how it’s processed. I enjoy taking things apart, understanding how they work, and finding ways to adapt or repair them. When I was younger, I didn’t have the opportunity to pursue that interest as a career. Now I can.

My electrical background first drew me towards the physical security of infrastructure. As an electrician in Auckland, I was surprised by how freely I could move around some buildings. People often opened doors with their access badges without asking what I was there to do. Sometimes, a tool belt and a ladder were enough to enter areas normally reserved for authorised people.

That showed me that security doesn’t depend only on technology. It also depends on everyday habits and the trust we place in others.

Through my studies, I discovered cybersecurity awareness. Understanding the risks and knowing how to protect ourselves can make a real difference, for a large business or simply for you and me at home. We all have a part to play.

What I bring to a team

Working with many different customers has taught me to handle technical challenges, tight deadlines and urgent situations. I know how to reassure people and manage stress. I’m keen to learn, I learn quickly, and I want to understand the things I work with.

I know why I’m making this career change. I’m determined, and I don’t give up. Experience has taught me to ask the right questions, listen to the answers and follow instructions. I also know how to find solutions independently, and I enjoy working on my own as much as being part of a team.

I’ve owned businesses and employed staff, so I understand the challenges potential clients face. My practical background also helps me act as a link between people in different roles within a business.

I’m looking for an entry-level opportunity in IT support, networking or cybersecurity in Auckland, where I can contribute my experience, learn from others and keep building my technical skills.
